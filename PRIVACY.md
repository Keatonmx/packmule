# Packmule privacy policy

Packmule has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry.

- Files move directly between your device and the servers **you** add (SMB or
  FTP). Packmule makes no other network connections.
- Server addresses are stored on your device. Passwords are stored in the iOS
  Keychain on your device.
- Bonjour discovery only listens for SMB/FTP services advertising on your own
  local network.
- Nothing is collected, sold or shared, because nothing ever leaves your
  device except the file transfers you start.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
